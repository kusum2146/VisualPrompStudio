Prompt A



Create a charcoal drawing on heavy-toothed grey paper that emphasizes strong contrast and organic shapes. Use willow charcoal sticks and kneaded erasers to execute a process of tonal layering and smudging. The composition features a gnarled, ancient oak tree in an open field, using rough texture to define the bark and creating a focal point through emphasis on the glowing light hitting the trunk. The edges are soft and smoky, showing the expressive, dusty qualities of the charcoal.

Prompt B



Create a linocut relief print printed with black oil-based ink on white paper, emphasizing strong contrast and organic shapes. Use u-shaped and v-shaped gouges to perform the process of relief carving, cutting away the negative space to leave bold, raised lines. The composition features the same gnarled, ancient oak tree from Prompt A, but translated into high-contrast black and white. Use rhythmic, carved marks to create a sense of rough texture in the wood and maintain emphasis on the light-colored trunk by carving away large areas of the linoleum block.
