Prompt:
Create a representational artwork in the medium of street mural painting.
Place the mural on the side of a public library in downtown Dallas, Texas.
The artwork should serve as a form of social protest by highlighting the issue of educational inequality.
Its social role is to inspire community discussion and encourage civic awareness about unequal access to learning resources.
The meaning of the artwork should reflect the message that education is a human right, and that disparities in funding, technology, and safety between neighborhoods must be addressed.
The subject matter should show:
A large open book glowing with light
Children of diverse backgrounds reaching toward it
Behind them, contrasting environments: one side showing a well-resourced school, and the other showing deteriorating buildings and outdated textbooks
Vocabulary 
Medium: Street mural painting
Meaning: Highlights educational inequality and advocates for equal access to learning.
Subject Matter: A glowing open book, children reaching toward it, and contrasting school environments.
Art Style: Representational art
